About Me & Academic Journey

Bridging classical veterinary medicine with modern computational biology, artificial intelligence, and evidence-based epidemiological surveillance.

Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) Student

Hello! I am Md. Nafizur Rahman, a dedicated Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) student at the Department of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh. My academic journey is built upon a strong foundation in Anatomy, Histology, Physiology, Pathology, Parasitology, Microbiology, Medicine, Surgery, Epidemiology, and Public Health. I am deeply passionate about bridging the gap between biological research and advanced computational science to improve animal welfare, public health, and disease prevention.

"The future of veterinary medicine won't be written at the microscope alone — it will be compiled at the intersection of biology and code."

Throughout my journey, I have integrated traditional veterinary science with modern Data Analytics, developing proficiency in Python, SQL, and data visualization tools like GeoPandas and Matplotlib to drive evidence-based research.

Beyond the lab coat, I am a self-driven developer who ships real products through "Vibe Coding" — an AI-assisted, rapid-prototyping workflow where a clear idea turns into a working app in days, not months. I am currently developing the 🩺 PoultryMedix mobile app and the ⚡ ZunSync veterinary software ecosystem.
